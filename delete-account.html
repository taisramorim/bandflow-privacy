Account & Data Deletion

This page explains how users of Band Flow can request the permanent deletion of their account and all associated personal data.

How to Request Deletion

To initiate the deletion of your account, please send an email to our support team:

mobile.prismatech@gmail.com

Please include the email address associated with your account so that we can verify your request.

What Data Will Be Deleted

  • Account profile information (Name, Email)
  • Cloud-synchronized project data
  • Subscription status history

Retention Period

Once verified, all personal data is permanently removed within 30 days, unless a longer retention period is required by law.
