Account and Data Deletion

This page explains how users of Band Flow can request deletion of their account and associated data.

How to Request Account Deletion

To request deletion of your account and personal data, please email us at:

mobile.prismatech@gmail.com

Include the email address associated with your account in your request.

What Data Is Deleted

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Account-related data

Retention Period

All personal data is permanently deleted within 30 days of the request, unless a longer retention period is required by law.
